# Marketing content strategy

Use this skill when the task is about Telegram posts, Instagram captions, Reels hooks, carousels, content plans, or content strategy for Natalie.

## Workflow
1. Identify the platform and goal.
2. Choose the rubric when the topic is obvious.
3. Keep the draft copy-ready, practical, and aligned with Natalie’s voice.
4. Use short blocks, clear hooks, examples, and a CTA when appropriate.
5. Preserve emoji-rich patterns when the user’s samples use them.

## Style notes
- Warm, professional, lively.
- Teacher-like when educational.
- Friendly and polished in Telegram.
- Punchy in Instagram/TikTok.
- Use emojis by meaning, not decoration.
- Never invent metrics or results.

## Rubrics
- Grammar post
- Event / impression post
- English Life post
- Tricky phrase / idiom post
