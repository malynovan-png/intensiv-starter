# Social brand system

Use this skill when the user asks to define, tighten, or apply a coherent visual and content brand across Instagram, Telegram, TikTok, or similar channels.

This skill helps translate brand taste into repeatable rules for palette, typography, layout, tone, and post packaging. It does not replace the content-writing skills; it supplies the design and identity layer they should follow.

## When to Use

- The user shares moodboards, palette ideas, font ideas, or visual references
- The user asks for a brand board, visual identity, or style guide
- The user wants social content to look and feel consistent across formats
- The user needs rules for color usage, typography, photo style, or layout rhythm
- The user wants a brand to feel recognizable without looking repetitive

## Core Process

1. Extract the identity words first: who the brand feels like, not just what colors it uses.
2. Reduce the visual system to a few repeatable anchors: 1 signature color, 1 supporting palette family, 1 headline style, 1 body style, 1 accent style.
3. Define how each element appears in practice: backgrounds, text, notes, frames, cards, photos, and small recurring details.
4. Name the “do more” and “do less” rules so the identity is repeatable by another person or future session.
5. Tie the visual system to content formats: Reels covers, carousels, Stories, worksheets, posts, and profile layout.

## Output Shape

When responding, give the user:
- a short identity summary
- a practical palette rule set
- typography rules
- photo / lifestyle direction
- recurring elements or motifs
- what not to do
- a one-line brand essence statement

## Important Rules

- Keep one color truly signature; do not let variants dilute it.
- Favor repeatable accents over full-frame saturation.
- Use typography contrast on purpose: editorial headline + clean body + small handwritten accent if appropriate.
- Avoid overdecorating. Recurrence creates memory; clutter creates noise.
- Separate identity from theme. A brand can be academic, modern, warm, and distinctive without leaning on clichés.
- Match the visual system to the user’s real content rhythm, not to a generic Pinterest template.

## Verification

Before sending, check that the brand system:
- has a clear signature element
- can be applied repeatedly
- fits the user’s actual content
- avoids unnecessary trend drift
- is specific enough to guide future posts and visuals
