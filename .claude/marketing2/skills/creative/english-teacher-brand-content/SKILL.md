# English Teacher Brand Content

Use this skill when the user asks for English-teaching content or branded education content across Telegram, Instagram, Reels, TikTok, Stories, carousels, captions, lesson sheets, or cover designs.

## When to Use

- Telegram posts for an English-teacher audience
- Instagram captions, carousels, and Stories
- Reels / TikTok hooks and short scripts
- lesson covers, worksheet layouts, cheat sheets, and printable materials
- prompts that need to match the user’s visual identity and tone

## Core approach

Build content from the user’s real life, role, and teaching context.

Use:
- first-person, personal framing when it fits
- a real problem, scene, or teaching moment as the hook
- short paragraphs and concrete examples
- a clear path from context to explanation to takeaway
- a soft CTA at the end

Do not write generic English-content filler. Keep the voice specific, lived-in, and naturally educational.

## Writing patterns

### Telegram posts
Use a personal case-study format:
- hook about a real situation
- short context
- 3-5 practical points or a short sequence
- a concise takeaway
- a soft CTA

### Instagram captions
Use a shorter, denser version of the same idea:
- a strong opening line
- 2-4 short paragraphs
- one clear lesson or insight
- a soft CTA

### Carousels
Use a clean editorial teaching structure:
- cover hook
- problem or context
- explanation
- example
- contrast or mini practice
- takeaway
- CTA

### Reels / TikTok
Use fast, modern openers:
- 1-2 line hook
- one teaching point
- short spoken rhythm
- no extra padding

## Visual language

When the user asks for design or cover prompts, keep the style consistent with the brand system:
- cream base
- burgundy #7B2636 as the main signature accent
- ink navy text
- serif headlines
- clean sans body text
- small handwritten accents
- lots of air
- editorial, academic, premium, and readable

For lesson materials, use a cream editorial worksheet look with burgundy titles, navy body text, rounded cards, and small handwritten notes.

For Telegram post design, use the same cream editorial system with rounded cards, burgundy accents, navy text, serif headers, and a premium English-exam feel.

For printable sheets, keep the design low-ink, clean, and highly readable.

## Pitfalls

- Do not turn the content into a generic language-school post.
- Do not use a different burgundy shade from the brand system.
- Do not overload with decorative elements.
- Do not make the tone too sterile or too childish.
- Do not over-explain when the user asked for a tight format.

## Verification

Before sending, check that the output:
- sounds like the user
- fits the requested platform
- has a clear hook and takeaway
- uses the brand’s visual language when design is involved
- stays specific rather than generic
