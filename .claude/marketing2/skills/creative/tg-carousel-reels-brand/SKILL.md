# TG Carousel / Reels Cover Brand Skill

Create three-slide English-teaching carousels and Reels covers in Natalie's recognisable brand system: modern American girl, Pinterest editorial, clean girl, educational scrapbook. The result must feel sophisticated, airy, and useful—not a generic language-school template.

## When to Use

Use for requests such as:
- «Задизайни мне это фото под карусель поста или обложку рилс с заголовком, например фразовые глаголы по теме “НАЗВАНИЕ ТЕМЫ” для ЕГЭ».
- «Сделай карусель в моём стиле по теме Education».
- Any request for a branded carousel, educational slide, or Reels cover using the user's photo.

Do not use for text-only captions, content plans, or unbranded generic illustrations.

## Brand System

- **Base:** cream / warm paper `#F7F2EA`.
- **Main text and structural blocks:** ink navy `#172A46`.
- **Signature accent:** burgundy `#7B2636`; never substitute it with a different red.
- **Secondary accent:** muted denim `#DCE6EF`.
- **Typography:** editorial high-contrast serif for headlines; clean sans serif for definitions and examples; natural neat handwritten accent only for small notes, underlines, and labels.
- **Reference execution — non-negotiable cover blueprint:** Treat the user’s *unaltered full-frame outdoor lifestyle photo* as the complete background, not as a framed photo inside a card. Build an asymmetric travel-journal collage directly on top of it:
  - Place a large irregular **torn cream-paper title sheet** across the mid-right of the frame. It must have visibly deckled/torn edges and a slight analogue paper texture—not a rounded rectangle.
  - Set the main Russian title in very large dark-burgundy, high-contrast editorial serif. Keep it compact and stacked on the paper sheet. Use a small handwritten script only for the line `по теме [TOPIC]`; place `для ЕГЭ` beneath in cream/white editorial serif directly on the photo.
  - In the upper-right, layer a small **black-and-white Polaroid/postcard** relevant to the topic, with a thin taped/washi-paper attachment and a hand-drawn postal cancellation / wavy postmark. It must overlap the photo and title zone.
  - Use white handwritten notes directly on the photo: a 3–4-line quote in the upper-left and a short aspirational note in the lower-right. Add only a few white doodles around them (heart, tiny plane, route line, globe, passport/luggage, location pin).
  - In the lower-left, place a neat vertical mini-list of three lifestyle benefits with simple thin white line icons; it should feel like a field-note annotation, not a UI card.
  - Use one restrained burgundy scribble/route stamp near the upper centre. Keep the composition intentionally layered, tactile, and slightly imperfect—like a personal travel scrapbook cover, not a tidy template.
- **Photo rule:** Do not crop out the person’s face or hands. Preserve the person, pose, styling, cup/object, and outdoor light exactly; paper and doodle elements may overlap the image but must never replace, retouch, or AI-alter the person.
- **Mood:** warm, sunlit American-girl travel journal; tactile paper, faded analogue details, postcard memories, handwritten study notes, and editorial education. This is the primary reference, not merely an optional mood.

Avoid: a boxed photo on a plain background; fully rounded cards; corporate grids; generic school-template layouts; flat vector illustrations; 3D icons; glossy gradients; neon; crowded random stickers; or overly clean minimalism.

## Interior-Slide Blueprint — Non-Negotiable

### Slide 2 — Key verbs / vocabulary
- Reproduce the supplied **Key verbs for Travelling** composition closely for every vocabulary slide: a warm textured paper page; a broad horizontal rough burgundy paint stroke near the top-left; large white editorial-serif `Key verbs` on the stroke; and an oversized black handwritten `for [TOPIC]` directly below with one outlined heart.
- Use a tall **10-row vertical vocabulary list** where possible. Each row has: a small beige numbered circle aligned on the left; a bold burgundy editorial-serif phrase; a small black em dash; then the Russian meaning in clean charcoal sans. Meanings may use a second line directly underneath, aligned with the definition—not with the phrase.
- Leave the list open and airy: no row boxes, separators, footer bars, grids, or coloured panels behind the vocabulary.
- In the upper-right, add a compact black handwritten note arranged in short lines (e.g. `Same verb / new meaning`) and one tiny outlined heart. In the right margin, add a small navy transport doodle (plane / car / train) with a looping dotted route. In the lower-right, place a 3–4-line black handwritten phrase such as `Phrasal verbs make your English sound natural` with a tiny heart.
- Build the bottom as an irregular postcard collage: a torn black-and-white/sepia landmark or travel photo entering from the lower-left; a small handwritten phrase like `Collect moments not things`; a hand-drawn globe / route mark; and a burgundy passport prop overlapping the lower-right. Keep the collage low enough that all 10 rows stay legible.
- This exact composition takes priority over generic vocabulary-slide guidance.

### Slide 3 — How to use them / application
- Use the same textured off-white paper page, burgundy / navy / cream system, and collage density as slide 2.
- Put the heading in white high-contrast serif on one wide **rough navy paintbrush stroke** at the top: `How to use them?`.
- Place a large black handwritten subtitle below, such as `3 простых шага`, plus one small outlined heart.
- Build exactly three vertically spaced steps. Each begins with a soft denim-blue numbered circle. Use a dark navy bold serif Russian step heading and a short clean dark-sans explanation beneath it.
- Place one small cream paper note taped with beige washi tape to the right of each step. Each note has a handwritten English example, then a small Russian explanation; notes must have slight rotation / imperfect placement and never become formal cards.
- Anchor the bottom with a wide torn vintage travel/landmark photograph. Layer a paperclip, a small handwritten note, and a burgundy brush-stroke encouragement label with white serif copy such as `You can do it!`.
- Keep visible negative space; decorations sit in margins and corners, never between rows of essential teaching text.

## Prerequisites

1. If the user provides a photo, treat it as the hero asset: **do not replace or alter the person, face, pose, clothing, or environment.**
2. Identify the requested topic and whether the user wants a full carousel or a Reels cover only.
3. If exact educational copy is supplied, preserve it exactly unless the user asks for rewriting.

## Procedure

1. **Set the content architecture.** Use one central teaching idea per slide. For a full carousel, make exactly three separate portrait images at 1080 × 1350 px, unless the user specifies another format.
2. **Build slide 1 — cover.** Follow the non-negotiable cover blueprint in **Brand System**. The supplied lifestyle photo fills the cover; the main title lives on an irregular torn cream paper sheet in the mid-right; the title system is burgundy editorial serif + small handwritten topic line + cream `для ЕГЭ` over the image. Add the Polaroid/postcard, postal mark, handwritten notes, lower-left icon list, and only the restrained doodles described there. Keep essential Reels-cover copy in the central safe area without covering the person’s face or hands.
3. **Build slide 2 — key vocabulary.** Follow **Interior-Slide Blueprint → Slide 2** exactly: textured paper, one rough burgundy brush heading, handwritten topic line, numbered vocabulary list, sparse travel doodles and handwritten marginal notes, then the torn-photo collage at the bottom. Do not use uniform row cards or a plain footer band.
4. **Build slide 3 — application.** Follow **Interior-Slide Blueprint → Slide 3** exactly: rough navy brush heading, handwritten Russian subtitle, three blue-circle steps, three imperfect taped example notes, and a torn landmark-photo collage with burgundy encouragement label at the bottom. Do not use uniform example cards.
5. **Export and deliver.** For carousels, deliver all three separate usable images. A triptych preview is optional and never replaces the three slides. For a Reels-cover-only request, produce slide 1 only.

## Layout Rules

- Hierarchy: photo/topic → educational value → decoration.
- Preserve generous negative space and safe margins for Instagram and Telegram crops.
- Slides 2 and 3 use the same header, grid, and margin system.
- Use rounded cards sparingly; do not turn every element into a pill.
- Decorations must never compete with headlines or lesson text.
- Always prioritise readability at a glance.

## Verification

Before delivering, inspect the rendered images with `vision_analyze` and confirm:
- all title, label, definition, and example text is readable;
- the burgundy is `#7B2636`, and the design is cream + navy led;
- the original supplied photo remains recognisably unaltered;
- the three slides are separate, consistently sized, and visually cohesive;
- no unsupported-font symbols or accidental placeholder glyphs appear;
- the carousel feels editorial, premium, and recognisably Natalie's brand.
