# Starlight 9 Test Maker — Reusable Skill Specification

## When to Use

Use when Natalie supplies a Starlight 9 spread and asks for Standard/Plus tests, parallel versions, answer keys, or a print-ready Word package.

## Purpose

Use this specification whenever the user supplies a new **Starlight 9** textbook spread (plus optional workbook, teacher's book, Grammar Reference or Vocabulary Bank pages) and asks for tests.

Create a print-ready set:
- Standard V1
- Standard V2
- Plus V1
- Plus V2
- Answer Key

The supplied Starlight pages are the primary source. Do not ask the user to repeat requirements already contained here.

## 1. Source priority

Use:
- vocabulary from **Check these words**;
- bold/highlighted vocabulary and phrases in the reading;
- vocabulary exercises on the spread;
- supplied Vocabulary Bank / Grammar Reference material;
- the grammar explicitly taught on the spread;
- reading facts only when Reading is requested.

Create new parallel items rather than mechanically copying textbook exercises. Do not add unrelated target vocabulary.

## 2. Four versions

V1 and V2 must test the same targets at comparable difficulty but must be genuinely different, not merely reordered copies. Change contexts, wording, item order and combinations.

## 3. STANDARD — default structure

### VOCABULARY — 15 points

**Exercise 1 — 5 points:** controlled vocabulary task such as matching words/phrases to definitions.

**Exercise 2 — 5 points:** Choose the correct word.

Example: **2 Choose the correct word. (5 points)**

1. Many people **take / make** having a permanent home for granted.

Bold only the alternatives themselves, including the slash. Do not bold the sentence around them.

**Exercise 3 — 5 points:** five gap-fill sentences with a bank of 8 items: **5 answers + 3 extra**. State: **There are THREE extra words/phrases.**

Mix confusables; do not place them in predictable pairs.

### GRAMMAR — 10 points

**Exercise 1 — 5 points:** Choose the correct form/tense. Five items. Bold only the alternatives.

**Exercise 2 — 5 points:** Complete with the correct form. Five items.

Default Standard total: **25 points**.

Do not add Reading to Standard unless explicitly requested.

## 4. PLUS — default structure

### VOCABULARY — 12 points

Use **one exercise only**:
- 12 sentences;
- 15-item word/phrase bank;
- exactly 3 extra;
- contextual discrimination;
- confusables mixed throughout rather than placed in obvious pairs.

### GRAMMAR — 12 points

Use **one exercise with 12 sentences**.

Plus must test subtler distinctions than Standard. Each item must be independently solvable from context.

### Critical rule for Stative/Dynamic Verbs

Use **one target blank per sentence**.

Do not write a sentence that contrasts two meanings of the same verb and therefore gives away the answer, e.g. avoid: “I ___ (think) he is right, but I ___ (think) about moving.”

Instead test those meanings in separate, non-adjacent items.

Use maximum reasonable verb variety and shuffle items so identical verbs do not occur consecutively. Where supported by the supplied material, draw from verbs such as: think, have, see, taste, smell, appear, fit, be, look, feel, weigh, enjoy, know, understand, believe, want, need, prefer, belong, own, contain, cost.

Only use Continuous when the context supports a dynamic meaning.

### Reading in Plus

Do **not** include Reading by default.

Approved Module 1b-style Plus structure: **Vocabulary 12 + Grammar 12 = Total 24.**

If Reading is explicitly requested, add it and recalculate the total.

## 5. Confusables

When the supplied spread contains confusables:
1. Use realistic contexts where learners genuinely confuse the words.
2. Do not arrange them as neat adjacent pairs.
3. Mix categories and order.
4. Avoid grammar clues that make the lexical answer obvious.
5. Ensure exactly one natural answer.
6. Follow distinctions represented in the supplied Starlight material.

Typical supplied examples may include shade/shadow; shake/nod; cultural/tradition/habit; trip/journey/travel/voyage/crossing.

## 6. Grammar quality

Before finalizing each grammar item:
- verify the answer is unambiguous;
- ensure no second form is equally natural;
- provide enough semantic/time context;
- avoid two blanks used merely to reveal a contrast;
- vary contexts and structures appropriate to the grammar point;
- avoid repeating the same target verb/contrast in adjacent items;
- make V1/V2 comparable but different.

Plus difficulty should come from meaning and context, not obscure wording.

## 7. Reading — only when requested

Use only facts supported by the supplied text. Preferred format: **T / F / NS**.

NS must genuinely be not stated, not merely false. Verify every key against the source.

## 8. Answer Key

Always include complete keys for:
- Standard V1
- Standard V2
- Plus V1
- Plus V2

Organize by exercise. For word banks, give numbered answers and list the 3 extra items separately. For grammar, give the exact expected form.

Prefer rewriting an ambiguous item rather than listing multiple acceptable answers.

## 9. Scores and grading

Show points in exercise headings, e.g. **2 Choose the correct word. (5 points)**

Do **not** append clutter such as `VOCABULARY — 15 points — TOTAL 25` or `GRAMMAR — 10 points — TOTAL 25`.

Section headings should simply be: **VOCABULARY** and **GRAMMAR**.

For a 30-point Plus test, when applicable:

**GRADING SYSTEM**
- 30 — 10
- 28–29 — 9
- 25–27 — 8
- 22–24 — 7
- 19–21 — 6
- 16–18 — 5

Never silently apply a grading scale whose maximum does not match the actual test total. Preserve a user-specified scale exactly or flag a mismatch before changing it.

## 10. Approved print layout

Final deliverable should normally be a **Word .docx ready for printing**.

Page:
- A4;
- duplex-friendly;
- two test variants on one A4 sheet;
- V1 on one half, V2 on the other;
- clear horizontal divider/cut line in the middle;
- corresponding V1/V2 positions consistent front/back.

### Standard

Preferred duplex:
- front: Standard V1 + V2 — Vocabulary;
- back: Standard V1 + V2 — Grammar.

### Plus

Preferred duplex:
- front: Plus V1 + V2 — Vocabulary;
- back: Plus V1 + V2 — Grammar.

Answer Key follows on separate teacher pages.

## 11. Approved typography

Mandatory unless explicitly overridden:
- **Times New Roman**;
- **12 pt**;
- body line spacing **1.15**;
- heading separation/spacing **1.5**;
- **all headings left-aligned**.

Do not center titles, section headings, exercise headings or Answer Key headings unless explicitly requested.

Do not silently shrink the entire test to make it fit.

### Bold rule

Bold:
- module/test title;
- section headings;
- exercise headings;
- only the answer alternatives in choose tasks.

Example: `1. Many people **take / make** having a permanent home for granted.`

Grammar example: `1. The Moken usually **live / are living** on their boats ...`

Do not bold the number or surrounding sentence.

## 12. Header style

Use concise headers such as: **Starlight 9. Module 1b — CONFUSABLES & STATIVE VERBS — STANDARD V1**

Then: **VOCABULARY** or **GRAMMAR**.

Do not repeat total points in section headings.

## 13. Word banks

Standard Exercise 3:
- 8 items total;
- 5 used + 3 extra.

Plus Vocabulary:
- 15 items total;
- 12 used + 3 extra.

Mix deliberately. Do not put correct answers first/extras last. Do not group confusable families together when this gives away distinctions.

## 14. Difficulty

### Standard

Focus on recognition, controlled choice, matching, clear gap fills and straightforward application.

### Plus

Require retrieval from a larger mixed bank, contextual distinction of close meanings, independent grammar selection and subtler semantic cues.

Remain faithful to Starlight 9 level and supplied materials.

## 15. Final quality-control checklist

### Content

- Four variants exist.
- All tested targets come from supplied materials.
- V1/V2 are genuinely different.
- Totals are correct.
- Exactly 3 extras appear where specified.
- Confusables are mixed.
- Grammar answers are unambiguous.
- Same target verbs/forms are not unnecessarily consecutive.
- Plus stative-verb items have one target blank per sentence.
- Answer Key matches final items.

### Formatting

- A4.
- Two variants per sheet.
- Cut line/divider in middle.
- Duplex positions align.
- Times New Roman 12.
- Body spacing 1.15.
- Heading spacing 1.5.
- All headings left-aligned.
- Only alternatives bold in choose tasks.
- No redundant `— points — TOTAL` after VOCABULARY/GRAMMAR headings.

### Print validation

Before delivery:
- render the finished DOCX;
- visually inspect every page;
- confirm no clipping;
- confirm no orphaned headings;
- confirm no exercise crosses the cut line incorrectly;
- confirm front/back V1/V2 halves align;
- confirm font was not silently reduced;
- confirm Answer Key is readable.

Do not call the file print-ready until this validation is complete.

## 16. Workflow for every new spread

1. Identify module number/title.
2. Identify target vocabulary.
3. Identify target grammar.
4. Review supplementary pages.
5. Build vocabulary inventory.
6. Build grammar contrast inventory.
7. Draft Standard V1/V2.
8. Draft Plus V1/V2.
9. Check V1/V2 parity.
10. Check Standard/Plus difficulty difference.
11. Create Answer Key.
12. Build DOCX in approved print layout.
13. Apply typography and alignment.
14. Render DOCX.
15. Inspect every page.
16. Fix overflow/alignment/content issues.
17. Deliver final DOCX.

## 17. Shortcut for future chats

If this file is supplied with a new spread and the user says: “Вот новый разворот Starlight 9. Сделай тесты по эталону.”, interpret this as an instruction to:
- analyze the supplied spread;
- create Standard V1/V2 and Plus V1/V2;
- follow this specification;
- create keys;
- package everything into the approved print-ready Word layout;
- render and visually inspect it;
- return the final DOCX.

Do not ask the user to restate these formatting rules.

## 18. Reference principle

The final approved **Starlight 9 Module 1b** formatting is the reference:
- Times New Roman 12;
- body 1.15;
- heading separation 1.5;
- all headings left;
- two variants per A4;
- middle cut;
- duplex-friendly Vocabulary/Grammar sides;
- no redundant point/total text after section headings;
- clean Answer Key;
- pedagogically selective bolding.

If a future request conflicts with this specification, the user's newest explicit instruction takes precedence.
