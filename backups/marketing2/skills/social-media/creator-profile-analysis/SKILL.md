# Creator profile analysis

Use when the user asks to review social profiles, identify what performs best, or turn creator patterns into ready Reels/TikTok scripts.

See `references/profile-analysis-notes.md` for the scorecard and output template.

## When to Use

- The user sends profile links, screenshots, or account names and wants the strongest posts.
- The user wants to know what makes a creator's content work.
- The user wants a remix into Reels scripts, hooks, or content ideas.
- The user wants to compare several creators in the same niche and borrow the best patterns.

## Goals

- Find the posts with the strongest visible signals.
- Explain why those posts work.
- Translate the pattern into the user's voice and niche.
- Return ready-to-use outputs, not just observations.

## Procedure

1. **Collect source material.**
   Prefer public profile pages, top posts, pinned posts, and visible metrics such as likes, views, comments, and saves if they are shown.

2. **Rank the posts.**
   Prioritize the strongest visible posts first. If exact metrics are hidden, use relative signals such as repeated attention, comment density, shares, or the platform's own ranking cues.

3. **Extract patterns.**
   Look for:
   - hook type
   - topic angle
   - pacing
   - persona
   - visual language
   - CTA style
   - lesson structure

4. **Translate, do not copy.**
   Borrow the mechanism, not the identity. Keep the user's niche, tone, and visual system intact.

5. **Deliver usable outputs.**
   Return:
   - top posts and why they worked
   - what to borrow
   - what to avoid
   - 3 to 10 ready Reels scripts or hooks
   - optional shot list, on-screen text, and CTA

## Pitfalls

- Do not invent metrics. If likes, views, or saves are not visible, say so and rely on the visible ranking signals.
- Do not overfit to one viral post. Look for repeated patterns across the strongest posts.
- Do not copy the creator's persona so closely that the user's brand disappears.
- If the profile is educational, separate attention value from teaching value. The most viral post is not always the best teaching model.
- If the account uses a strong lifestyle persona, translate the energy into the user's own style instead of mimicking the exact voice.

## Verification

- Confirm the source posts used.
- Confirm why each selected post is strong.
- Confirm the final scripts fit the user's niche and tone.
- If metrics were unavailable, state that clearly and explain which public signals were used instead.
