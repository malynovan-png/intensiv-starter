# Starlight → Exam Content

Use this skill when Natalie sends a Starlight 9 or Starlight 10 spread and asks what to take for OGE/EGE or to turn that selection into Telegram, Reels, TikTok or carousel content. The goal is not to retell the textbook: analyse, filter and activate the language that will matter on the exam.

## When to Use

- Natalie sends photos/scans of a Starlight 9 or Starlight 10 unit.
- She asks what vocabulary, grammar or phrases are worth taking from a unit.
- She asks for a post, Reels script, carousel or lesson-content idea based on a spread.
- She asks how a unit connects to OGE or EGE.

Do not use this skill to produce a complete unit summary or a generic vocabulary list.

## Core Positioning

Use this idea consistently:

> We do not simply go through Starlight. From every unit, we analyse and take into active use the language that will help on the exam.

### Starlight 9 → OGE

The exam is at the end of the school year. Filter material for:

- grammar tasks **20–28**;
- word formation tasks **29–34**;
- oral part: dialogue/interview and project monologue;
- electronic personal email;
- vocabulary needed to understand listening and reading.

Important: OGE does **not** have a direct equivalent of EGE lexical-choice tasks 30–36. Do not describe phrasal verbs or collocations as direct OGE test tasks. They are still valuable for oral answers, email, listening and reading.

### Starlight 10 → EGE

This is a two-year preparation journey. Filter material for:

- lexical tasks **30–36**: context, collocations, polysemy, phrasal verbs, speech clichés and logical links;
- word formation **25–29**;
- Speaking 3 interview answers;
- writing;
- grammar points where candidates often lose marks.

## Official Evidence Standard

Do not claim a word is “the most frequent on the exam” without verifiable recurrence in the FIPI open bank or open variants.

Base exam claims on current FIPI materials:

- EGE: https://fipi.ru/ege/demoversii-specifikacii-kodifikatory
- EGE open bank: https://fipi.ru/ege/otkrytyy-bank-zadaniy-ege
- OGE: https://fipi.ru/oge/demoversii-specifikacii-kodifikatory
- OGE Navigator: https://doc.fipi.ru/navigator-podgotovki/navigator-oge/MR_angl_yaz_oge_2026.pdf

Use honest wording:

- “полезно для…”
- “может встретиться в…”
- “поможет ответить на…”
- “типичная зона ошибок…”

Never invent frequency rankings, exam statistics or FIPI wording.

## Exam-Selection Framework

When Natalie sends a spread, identify language in four buckets.

### 1. EXAM CORE

Take first:

- frequent collocations and fixed combinations;
- phrasal verbs with a usable real-life meaning;
- word families and affixes;
- polysemous words and confusing pairs;
- grammar structures with a known error risk;
- thematic vocabulary that a student can actively use.

### 2. SPEAKING & WRITING

Prioritise language that turns into a complete answer:

- precise verbs and adjectives;
- opinions, reasons, preferences and comparisons;
- polite questions;
- discourse markers;
- phrases that extend an answer naturally without sounding memorised.

### 3. EXAM TRAP

Mark units that can cause a predictable error:

- part of speech vs word form;
- negative prefix;
- singular/plural signal;
- verb tense or voice;
- gerund/infinitive;
- pronoun or comparative form;
- lexical compatibility or phrasal-verb particle.

### 4. EXTRA

Interesting textbook language that is not a first priority for the exam. Do not overload the content with it.

## Unit Analysis Procedure

1. **Identify the branch.** Confirm whether the material is Starlight 9 → OGE or Starlight 10 → EGE. Completion: the exam target is named before selection begins.
2. **Read the full spread first.** Note the topic, vocabulary boxes, grammar focus, speaking prompts, writing tasks and examples. Completion: do not select isolated words without context.
3. **Select 3–7 high-value units.** Prefer useful combinations over long lists of single words. Completion: every selected item has a clear reason to stay.
4. **Label each unit.** Use `EXAM CORE`, `SPEAKING & WRITING`, `EXAM TRAP` or `EXTRA`. Completion: each item has one primary use.
5. **Connect it to an exam action.** State exactly where and how it helps: a task number, oral answer move, email sentence or grammar pattern. Completion: no vague “will be useful for the exam”.
6. **Create one natural example.** Use an age-appropriate, spoken-English example rather than textbook-sounding filler. Completion: a student can say it aloud.
7. **Choose the best content angle.** Do not force every selection into every platform. Completion: the output has one clear educational promise.

Use this compact format unless Natalie asks for a different one.

```markdown

   - **Example:** [natural sentence]

   - **Where it helps:** [specific exam use]
   - **Exam trap / note:** [brief clarification]
```

## Series Opening Hook

Open Starlight → OGE/EGE Reels with a recurring line that connects school material to the exam. The message: we do not merely cover the textbook; we collect an active, useful exam bank from it.

Use a short date-led hook by default. It must feel like an invitation, not a methodology lecture:

- `Сдаёшь ОГЭ в [YEAR]? Собираем полезное из Starlight 9.`
- `Starlight 9: что забираем для ОГЭ-[YEAR].`
- `Starlight 9: из каждого юнита берём то, что реально пригодится на ОГЭ.`
- `Сдаёшь ЕГЭ в [YEAR]? Собираем полезное из Starlight 10.`
- `Starlight 10: что забираем для ЕГЭ-[YEAR].`

Then state the specific promise of the Reel in one short line.

## Recurring Content Rubrics

### Starlight 10 → Speaking 3

**Promise:** “Фразы из этого юнита, которые помогут не зависнуть на интервью.”

Structure:

1. Context hook: `Starlight 10, Unit [X]. Эти фразы помогут отвечать на Speaking 3 уверенно, а не односложно.`
2. Give 3–5 phrases with translation.
3. Show one typical interview question.
4. Give a natural 2–3-sentence answer using the selected language.
5. Finish: `Не учим фразы отдельно — сразу учимся отвечать ими.`

### Starlight 10 → Use of English

**Promise:** “Что из Unit [X] стоит забрать для 30–36.”

Structure:

1. One collocation, phrasal verb, confusing pair or link.
2. Explain the context and typical trap.
3. Give a short four-option or gap-style mini task only if the answer is verified.
4. Explain why the answer fits the context.

### Starlight 10 → Grammar Check

**Promise:** “Грамматика из юнита, на которой выпускники часто теряют баллы.”

Structure:

1. Common incorrect form.
2. Short correction and reason.
3. EGE-style micro-example.
4. One speaking/writing sentence that activates the form.

### Starlight 9 → OGE Grammar

**Promise:** “Что из Unit [X] поможет в заданиях 20–28.”

Structure:

1. Grammar point from the spread.
2. Typical error.
3. OGE-style gap.
4. Clear one-sentence rule and active example.

### Starlight 9 → OGE Word Builder

**Promise:** “Одно слово из Unit [X] — несколько форм для 29–34.”

Structure:

1. Word family: `base → noun → adjective → adverb → negative form` where natural.
2. Explain the signal in the sentence that determines the needed part of speech.
3. Show one concise OGE-style gap.
4. Name the trap: suffix, prefix, spelling or number.

### Starlight 9 → OGE Speaking / Writing

**Promise:** “Что из Unit [X] поможет говорить и писать без заученного текста.”

Use selected thematic phrases in:

- a compact dialogue/interview answer;
- a project-monologue point;
- one or two email sentences or questions.

## Platform Adaptation

### Reels / TikTok

Use author-1 energy as the default: light, clear, useful, one idea at a time.

- Hook: 1–2 natural lines.
- Explain one small problem, not the full rule.
- Put the exam use on screen early.
- Use a mini-example.
- Keep spoken rhythm and avoid academic wording.

Example:

> `В Starlight 10 это просто новая фраза. Но на Speaking 3 она спасает от ответа “Yes, I do”.`

### Telegram

For Starlight revision posts, use a compact one-post cheat-sheet. The goal: a student opens it before a quiz, quickly repeats and writes the test. Never split one topic into 1/2 and 2/2 unless Natalie explicitly asks.

Structure:

- exact heading: `Starlight [9/10]. Module [X]_[section] [topic]`;
- one short question/promise;
- each item: emoji + term → `Зачем / meaning` → key collocation or form → one short example with Russian translation;
- a short `Проверка` when a contrast can be decided through one question;
- a two-to-four-line `Итог`.

Formatting rules:

- use short blocks, bold terms and `FORM:` / `Ex:` labels where useful;
- render every English example and its Russian translation as a Telegram blockquote (`>`);
- use only `🔹`, `🟣`, `🟢`, `👉🏻`, `❗️`, `🩷` as bullets or visual markers, and only where they add structure;
- use `---` only to separate major contrasts;
- no tables, long theory, unnecessary introductions or loud CTA;
- keep it ready to publish with minimal editing.

### Carousel

Use only when visual comparison adds value:

1. cover: Unit + exam promise;
2. selected language / grammar;
3. application: question, answer or exam mini-task.

## Voice and Editorial Balance

### Base style: author 1

For all Starlight → exam content:

- light, clear, specific and useful;
- explain without sounding “methodological” or heavy;
- student-friendly Russian with natural English fragments when useful;
- one practical takeaway per post.

### Accent style: authors 2 and 3

Use for a small dose of humour, personality, nostalgia and playful delivery. Do not let it obscure the learning point.

### Friday / Saturday relief content

Use **English on Screen — How they really say it** for entertaining series-based English:

`context trigger → phrase → playful false impression → real meaning → mini case → memorizer → soft CTA`.

This content supports the educational system but is not an exam drill.

## Quality Check

Before delivery, verify:

- [ ] The content names the correct exam and task type.
- [ ] Selected material comes from the supplied spread, unless Natalie asks for additions.
- [ ] Every item has an application, not just a translation.
- [ ] OGE and EGE formats are not mixed up.
- [ ] No unverified claim calls a word “most frequent”.
- [ ] Examples are natural, age-appropriate and easy to say aloud.
- [ ] The explanation is light and clear, not overloaded.
- [ ] The draft is ready for a quick publish edit.
