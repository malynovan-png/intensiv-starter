# Marketing Content Strategy

Use when the user asks for marketing content, content planning, reels, carousels, soft-selling, audience growth, or a content calendar for Telegram, Instagram, or TikTok.

## What this skill does
- Generates post ideas, hooks, headlines, CTAs, and content plans.
- Drafts Telegram posts and Instagram captions in a warm expert tone.
- Creates Reels scripts, carousel outlines, and soft-selling copy.
- Chooses the content format based on the topic and platform.
- Keeps output copy-ready and practical.

## Default workflow
1. Identify the platform and goal.
2. Infer the best format when it is obvious.
3. If the format is ambiguous, ask one short clarifying question.
4. Draft with clear structure: hook → body → example → CTA.
5. Keep tone natural, professional, and concise.
6. Use the user's established style guide for recurring rubrics.

## Style rules
- Write in Russian by default.
- Mirror mixed Russian/English prompts lightly when needed.
- Prefer short paragraphs and bullet lists.
- Avoid generic marketing fluff.
- Do not invent results, metrics, or analytics.
- Do not publish or send anything without approval.

## Content patterns
- Educational Telegram posts: clear rule, examples, takeaway, CTA.
- Instagram captions: stronger hook, tighter body, visual rhythm.
- Reels: short hook, beat list, shot notes, closing CTA.
- Carousels: slide-by-slide structure, one idea per slide.
- Soft-selling: expertise first, offer second.
